About Numera

"Don't just solve math. See it." Founded by Mubashir Shah.

Static textbook diagrams

Fixed, non-interactive visuals that can't adapt to how a given student thinks.

Long lecture videos

Passive content that doesn't respond to individual confusion or pace.

Symbol-heavy equations

Abstract notation without visual grounding leaves students lost.

One-size-fits-all explanations

Generic teaching that ignores how each student actually learns.

❝Every year, millions of students learn mathematics by memorizing procedures instead of building intuition — understanding, not memorization, is the missing layer in STEM education.

We're a new startup — small team, early product, and honest about both. Numera started from a simple annoyance: the best way to actually understand an equation is to watch it move, but building that animation by hand in Manim takes hours even for someone who knows the library well. So we're teaching a model to do it instead.

Today Numera has been trained on 19 equations, spanning algebra, trigonometry, calculus, and a few things further out — enough to prove the core idea works: type a formula, get back a scene, no code required.

We're currently raising our seed round to take Numera from a 19-equation prototype to a model that can visualize almost anything a student, tutor, or teacher throws at it.

Docs & FAQ

Good to know.

How many equations does Numera actually support right now?
We've hand-trained and verified 19 equations so far — they're all listed in the Templates section above. Anything outside that set can still be plotted in the Playground, but it's marked as experimental.
What is Manim, and why does it matter?
Manim is the animation engine originally built for 3Blue1Brown-style math videos. It produces clean, precise animations, but writing scenes by hand takes real time and Python experience. Numera writes that code for you.
Can I export and reuse the animations?
Yes. Free accounts export at 720p, Creator and Classroom plans export at 1080p, and you're free to use the renders in lectures, videos, or coursework.
Do you have an API?
Not publicly yet — it's on the roadmap for after our seed round closes. If you want early API access, mention it in the contact form below.
I'm a teacher. Can I use this in class?
That's exactly who Classroom is built for — multi-seat access and shareable links for your students. Reach out through the form below and we'll set your school up directly.
